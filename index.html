About the studio

VNGame Studio is a small, product-focused team based in Hanoi. We design, build and operate our own apps end to end — from sound design and gameplay to live operations and user acquisition.

Our apps are played in markets including the United States, Japan, South Korea and Taiwan. We keep them free to use, supported by advertising, and update them continuously based on player feedback.
